# Money

**Voice:** Clear. Unhurried.
**Delivery:** End on the last line with quiet weight. Do not moralise.

---

Money is stored freedom.

It is the ability to say no.

[pause · half beat]

Spend less than you earn.

The gap is where freedom lives.

Debt is a quiet chain.

Build things that pay you back.

[pause · 1 beat]

Looking wealthy and being wealthy are not the same.

One is loud.

The other is patient.

[pause · 1 beat]

Respect money. Do not worship it.

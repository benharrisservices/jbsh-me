# Leverage

**Voice:** Strategic. Clear.
**Delivery:** Speak the four kinds slowly, as if placing them on a table.

---

Hard work is necessary.

It is not enough.

[pause · 1 beat]

There are four kinds of leverage.

People. Money. Code. Words.

[pause · half beat]

Two of them cost you nothing to start.

[pause · half beat]

Code works while you sleep.

Words travel while you rest.

[pause · 1 beat]

Build things that work without you.

Write things that teach without you.

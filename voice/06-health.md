# Health

**Voice:** Honest. Caring. Never alarmist.
**Delivery:** The single-word lines need space. Walk. Lift. Eat. Guard.

---

You cannot build from a body that is failing.

Health compounds both ways.

[pause · half beat]

You do not need to be an athlete.

You need consistency.

[pause · 1 beat]

Walk.

Lift something heavy.

Eat real food.

Guard your sleep.

[pause · half beat]

Everything runs on rest.

[pause · 1 beat]

Your body is the only place you have to live.
